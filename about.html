About Rxpress Transportation

Who We Are

Rxpress Transportation is a Wisconsin-based non-emergency medical transportation provider dedicated to delivering safe, reliable, and professional transportation services.

10+ Years Experience Wisconsin-Based MTM Compliant
Our Story

Built on Experience,
Driven by Compassion

With over 10 years of experience in the transportation industry, we understand the importance of timely service, clear communication, and compassionate care for every passenger. Our team is committed to supporting individuals who rely on transportation to access medical appointments, essential services, and daily living needs.

We take pride in serving our community with integrity, accountability, and professionalism.

Why We Started

We saw too many people miss critical appointments dialysis, therapy, check-ups, life-saving treatments simply because they lacked reliable rides. We knew we could do better, so we created a service focused on getting people there safely, comfortably, and on time.

Our Commitment

We promise punctuality, clear communication, safety, and professionalism on every trip. Each client is treated with respect and care because we understand how important these appointments are to their health and well-being.

Community Focus

We're proud to serve individuals and families across Wisconsin. Our goal is to remove transportation barriers and ensure every passenger has access to the care and services they need. Every ride is a chance to make someone's day easier that's what drives us.

Our Mission

What We're Here to Do

At Rxpress Transportation, our mission is to provide safe, reliable, and compliant non-emergency medical transportation services in partnership with healthcare networks, insurance providers, and contracted partners ensuring every passenger receives timely and compassionate care.

Medical Appointment Transportation

Dependable transportation to and from medical appointments, ensuring no patient misses critical care.

Hospital Discharges & Follow-Ups

Safe, timely rides for hospital discharges and follow-up visits handled with care and professionalism.

Routine & Recurring Scheduling

Consistent scheduling for dialysis, therapy, and ongoing care built into every client's routine.
